Consulting begins with understanding the client’s situation and identifying relevant questions and areas for consideration. Each service below is a proposed starting point for discussion. A real engagement’s scope, deliverables, and measures of progress would be established with the client.

01
Proposed service

Management consulting

Structured support for leadership teams addressing important organizational questions. Work may include clarifying the challenge, examining relevant information, and helping leaders compare potential responses.

  • Shared framing of the issue
  • Clearer decision considerations
  • Recommendations shaped to context

Business strategy

Facilitated examination of an organization’s direction, priorities, and options. The process can help teams articulate assumptions, consider alternatives, and align choices with agreed objectives.

  • Defined strategic questions
  • Visible priorities and trade-offs
  • A basis for coordinated decisions

Business development

Support to explore potential growth pathways, partnership options, and capability needs. Any market or opportunity assessment would depend on client-supplied context and agreed research scope.

Proposed service

Operational improvement

Review of workflows and operational challenges to understand where work encounters friction and what changes may be worth evaluating. Recommendations would be developed with people familiar with the work.

Proposed service

Consideration of roles, decision pathways, structures, and ways of working as organizational needs change. The work would be tailored to the context and people involved.

Frequently asked questions

Some useful
starting points.

Illustrative answers. Confirm company-specific scope and details before publishing.

What does a management consultant do?

A consultant may help an organization understand a business question, assess relevant information, and consider possible recommendations. The actual work depends on the client and agreed scope.

How does the consulting process begin?

It can begin with a conversation about the organization’s goals, challenges, and expectations. A formal engagement would require an agreed scope and terms.

Can consulting be tailored to a specific business?

Any proposed work should be discussed in the context of the organization’s priorities, resources, market, and operating situation.

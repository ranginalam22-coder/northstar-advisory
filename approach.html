A collaborative process

Thoughtful steps.
Clearer direction.

A proposed process to understand the situation, develop options, and support considered action.

How it works

Work shaped
around your context.

The sequence below is a flexible framework, not a promise of specific results or a fixed engagement. Scope, timing, participants, deliverables, and evaluation measures would be agreed before work begins.

01
Step one

Initial consultation

Discuss business objectives, challenges, expectations, and the potential scope. This first conversation helps establish which questions and information should guide the work.

02
Step two

Business assessment

Review relevant business information and processes. Identify strengths, areas requiring attention, assumptions, and possible opportunities for further investigation.

03
Step three

Strategic planning

Define priorities, consider possible options, and shape recommendations and objectives with the client, taking available resources and context into account.

04
Step four

Implementation

Organize agreed recommendations into practical next steps. Implementation support is only included when defined in the approved project scope.

05
Step five

Performance evaluation

Where included, review progress against objectives and measures agreed with the client, then discuss lessons or potential adjustments.

Working principles · proposed

Good work depends
on how we work.

Proposed principles, subject to client review and approval.

01

Client collaboration

Involve relevant people in framing the work and considering options.

02

Clear communication

Keep questions, assumptions, and recommendations easy to discuss.

03

Practical thinking

Take available capacity and operating context into consideration.

04

Evidence-informed decisions

Make clear what information informs the discussion and what remains uncertain.

05

Continuous improvement

Allow for reflection and adaptation where the scope includes review.

Tailored consulting

Solutions built around
your business.

Recommendations should reflect the organization’s industry, objectives, operating situation, resources, and challenges. The process is intended to make room for that context rather than apply the same answer to every business.

Client collaboration

Open communication, relevant information sharing, agreed project objectives, and a chance to review recommendations help maintain clarity throughout an engagement.

Engagement note. Scope, project responsibilities, implementation assistance, review measures, and any follow-up need to be confirmed in an approved agreement before work begins.
